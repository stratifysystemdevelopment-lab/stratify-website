Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our development process.

How long does it take to build a system? +
It depends on the complexity. Standard payroll or accounting systems take about 3 to 6 weeks, while large custom enterprise builds can take 2 to 3 months.
Is the system cloud-based or installed locally? +
We highly recommend and build cloud-based web applications so you can access them securely anywhere. However, local network deployment is possible upon request.
Do you offer after-sales support or maintenance? +
Yes, we provide free bug-fixing post-launch. After that, we offer affordable monthly maintenance packages for updates and server management.
